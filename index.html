      How This Works

      Stuck between a few options? Lay them out, decide what actually matters, and let the math settle it.

      1. Name your decision
        A short title so you remember what you're comparing later.
      2. Add your options
        The things you're actually choosing between.
      3. Add your criteria
        What matters for this decision: cost, time, risk, whatever is relevant. Give each one a weight from 1 to 5 based on how much it matters to you.
      4. Score each option
        Rate every option 1 to 10 against each criterion. Be honest, not optimistic.
      5. Read your ranking
        Your options are automatically ranked by weighted score, highest to lowest, no spreadsheet required.
      Not sure what criteria to use? If you're sizing up a business idea, MJ DeMarco's "The Millionaire Fastlane" judges them against five things: Control, Entry, Need, Time, and Scale (CENTS). Basically asking: do you own it, is it hard to copy, does anyone need it, does it need you forever, and can it grow. That's a solid starting point for that kind of call, but the criteria here are entirely yours: job offers, apartments, colleges, whatever you're weighing.
